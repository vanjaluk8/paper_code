Taxonomy of Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning

Additive methods

Insert new trainable parameters into or alongside the frozen backbone.
Bottleneck adapters
Houlsby 2019, Pfeiffer 2021
Prefix tuning
Li & Liang 2021, Lester 2021

Reparameterisation methods

Decompose the weight update into low-rank or quantised factors.
LoRA (Hu 2022)
QLoRA (Dettmers 2023)
DoRA (Liu 2024)

Selective methods

Update a fixed subset of existing backbone parameters.
BitFit (Ben Zaken 2022)
Diff Pruning (Guo 2021)

Trade-off: additive methods add the most inference-time overhead but are the most modular (swappable per task); reparameterisation methods (LoRA family) add zero inference overhead after weight merging; selective methods add no new parameters but require access to update the backbone directly.
