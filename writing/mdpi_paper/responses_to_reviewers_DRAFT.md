## Reviewer 1 (methods / rigour / reproducibility)

### Overall assessment (R1, verbatim)
> "This is a substantial, well-organised SLR with a genuinely transparent and auditable data trail... However, several methodological points must be resolved before publication: the PRISMA flow is presented as a monotonic funnel even though one stage increases (464→552), the merge arithmetic in §3.1 is loosely expressed, and the paper relies on a circular arXiv-inclusion criterion and single-reviewer screening without an inter-rater reliability measure. Publishable after major revision."

---

**R1-1 — PRISMA funnel not monotonic; §3.1 merge arithmetic imprecise.**
> "…552 is *larger* than 464… Reporting this as a single descending chain misrepresents the process. Separately, §3.1 reads 'After deduplication, **972 unique records** entered title screening, which merged with the 352-paper pre-validated corpus…to a **502**-record corpus' — but the validated files show only the 162 screened-INCLUDE records merge with 352 (162+352=514→502), not all 972."

**Response:** Corrected. (1) §3.1 now states the merge precisely: "after deduplication, **972** unique records entered title screening, of which **162** passed; these screened-in records were merged with the 352-paper pre-validated corpus (G0–G6) to a **502**-record corpus." (2) The 464→552 increase is now explicit in both §3.1 and the Figure 5 caption: the abstract-review pool (**552**) is the 464 enriched records **plus 88 records added by a supplementary forward-citation snowball pass** (Scopus/WoS), i.e., a distinct identification branch rather than a sequential decrease. *(Files: `03_methodology.tex` §3.1 and Fig. 5 caption.)*

**R1-2 — Circular arXiv-inclusion criterion.**
> "Appendix A.3 admits the acceptance threshold for preprints is 'cited by at least one peer-reviewed work in the corpus'… This cannot validate inclusion independently and could systematically bias the corpus… *Requested action:* Replace the in-corpus citation proxy with a corpus-independent criterion… and quantify how many of the 18 arXiv-source records entered solely via the circular rule."

**Response:** We acknowledge this limitation explicitly in §11.3 ("Threats to validity"), where it is flagged as a circularity in the arXiv-inclusion proxy. A corpus-independent replacement (external citation count via Semantic Scholar/Crossref, or a venue-quality threshold) and a quantification of the at-risk records are **planned but not yet applied** — they require current citation counts for the 123 records, which is the outstanding **D4** input. **[OPEN — awaiting D4.]**

**Response:** The single-reviewer design is disclosed as a threat (§11.3). A calibration protocol — independent re-screening of a ~10% random subset and reporting % agreement / Cohen's κ in §3.1, with PRISMA item 8 updated — is **planned but requires the second reviewer's actual decisions**, the outstanding **D3** input. **[OPEN — awaiting D3.]**

**R2-1 — Quality rubric conflates relevance and bibliographic proxies with quality.**
> "Three of the six dimensions are not research-quality indicators: Q1 is venue prestige, Q2 is record resolvability, and Q6 is the *relevance tier*… Scoring a paper higher because it is more on-topic is circular… *Requested action:* Rename/reframe the instrument as reporting/evidence completeness and relevance, and either drop Q6 or report dimensions disaggregated."

**Response:** Reframed. The §3.3 instrument is now described as appraising "the **evidence quality** of each record" and, explicitly, "measur[es] the **reporting completeness** of each record and its **relevance to this synthesis**, not the intrinsic scientific merit of the underlying study." Q1/Q2/Q6 are named as bibliographic/relevance proxies, and the scoped-out classic quality dimensions (code availability, reproducibility, baseline adequacy, threat reporting) are stated as un-populated and disclosed rather than estimated. *(File: `03_methodology.tex` §3.3.)*
